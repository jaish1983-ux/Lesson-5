💪 Why Your Body Needs Exercise

Your body is like a supercar: the more you use it, the stronger it becomes. However, don't forget the main factor—nutrients.

Nutrients play a big role in repairing muscles and providing enough energy.

Quick Fact: Human blood vessels span 60,000 – 100,000 miles end-to-end.

✨ Quick Fascinating Facts

  • ❤️ The human heart generates its own electrical impulses, causing it to beat about 100,000 times a day.
  • 🩸 Red blood cells carry oxygen through over 60,000 miles of blood vessels.
  • 🔄 The stomach lining replaces itself every few days to avoid digesting itself, and the liver can regrow lost tissue.
  • ⚡ Your cells turn food into ATP energy to power 100,000+ chemical reactions every second.
  • 🧬 Your body makes about 2 million new red blood cells every second to keep oxygen flowing.
